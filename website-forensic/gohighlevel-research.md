## Overview
GoHighLevel (abbreviated GHL, also "HighLevel") is an all-in-one CRM and marketing
automation platform built for marketing agencies, consultants, and service-based
businesses (SMBs). It consolidates tools that are normally separate: CRM, email
marketing, SMS, funnels, website builder, scheduling, automation, reputation
management, and white-label SaaS — into a single dashboard.

Positioning: replaces 10-15 separate tools (e.g., Salesforce + Mailchimp +
ClickFunnels + Calendly + Zapier). Starting price cited ~$97/month.

## Feature Categories (high-level)
- CRM & contact management (360-degree contact records, unlimited contacts)
